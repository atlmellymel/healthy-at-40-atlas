Healthy at 40 — Foundations

Sleep

Consistent, sufficient sleep is a foundational component of recovery, cognition and metabolic health.

Nutrition

Build the foundation around adequate nutrition, protein, fiber, micronutrients and hydration rather than relying on experimental compounds.

Strength

Progressive resistance training supports muscle, bone and functional capacity across aging.

Cardiovascular fitness

Regular aerobic activity supports cardiovascular and metabolic health.

Mobility

Maintain useful joint range of motion and movement capacity.

Stress management

Breathing, relaxation, social connection, music and other behavioral strategies can complement broader health practices.
